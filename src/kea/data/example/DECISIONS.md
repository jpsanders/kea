### D-0001 · {{d:-70}} · Close the market stall after October

- **Decision:** Run the Saturday market stall to the end of October, then stop.
- **Status:** COMPLETED
- **Why:** Two staff for six hours sold EUR 310 on a good day; the stall did not cover its labour.
- **Revisit:** {{d:-10}}
- **Outcome:** Closed after the last October market. The season lost EUR 640 once labour was counted.
- **Surprise:** 4
- **Lesson:** Price any weekend venture by the hour of labour, not by the takings.
- **Revisited:** {{d:-8}}
