## 1. The situation in one paragraph

Tidewater Coffee has one cafe making EUR 21,900 a month and a roaster running at 40% of capacity. Cash on hand is
EUR 18,400. A second site is on offer at EUR 2,350 a month for five years, with three months' deposit. Two cafes buy
wholesale, 140 kg a month. Staff hours available are UNVERIFIED.

## 2. The decision being made

**Decision:** sell the roaster's spare capacity wholesale for 30 days before deciding on the second site.

**Alternatives considered and rejected:**

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| Sign the lease now | Commits EUR 141,000 over five years on an untested second location |
| Do nothing | The roaster's spare 60% earns nothing |
