# LEARNING: what reality taught the brain

Written when a plan closes or a prediction surprises. One entry per lesson. Seats on the council are scored here too:
after three cycles you know which ones to keep.

## Entry template

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD: <topic>
**Expected:** what the plan or the prediction said, with its confidence
**Happened:** what actually happened, with its source
**Surprise:** 0 to 10
**Seats:** which council seats called it, which missed it
**Changes:** what is now different in STATE.md, DECISIONS.md or a framework
```
