# PREDICTIONS

Falsifiable, dated, with a confidence, and scored when the date comes. This file is how the brain learns: after a few
dozen, `kea calibration` shows whether your 80% means 80%.

- Make one: `kea predict "Two of the five pilots convert to paid" --by +60d --confidence 40`
- Score it: `kea score P-0001 true --note "invoice 2027-014 paid"` (true, false, partial or void)

A good prediction names a number and a date, and anyone could score it without asking what you meant.
