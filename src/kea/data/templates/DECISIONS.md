# DECISIONS

Every significant decision, with why it was made and when to look at it again. Unlogged reasoning does not compound.

- Log one: `kea decide "Open on Sundays" --decision "Open 9 to 14 from 1 December" --why "..." --revisit +90d`
- When its date comes: `kea revisit D-0001 --outcome "..." --surprise 3 --lesson "..."`
- Edit entries by hand freely. Keep the heading line and the `- **Field:** value` lines, and kea keeps reading them.
